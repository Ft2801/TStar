# Inspiration

TStar has been heavily inspired by and has drawn some of its functionalities, algorithms, and workflows from other incredible astrophotography software. We would like to express our gratitude and acknowledge the following projects:

## Siril
[Siril](https://siril.org/) is an astronomical image processing tool created to process images. Some of the features and processing methodologies in TStar have been implemented taking inspiration from the workflow, capabilities, and the work of the Siril team. 
*Important License Note:* Siril is distributed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)**. TStar incorporates code from Siril and is released entirely under the terms of the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).

## Seti Astro Suite
[Seti Astro Suite](https://github.com/setiastro/setiastrosuitepro) provides various tools for astrophotography. Certain contents, dialogs, and features within TStar have been inspired by or derived from Seti Astro Suite, and are fully integrated and released under the terms of the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).

## RapidRAW
[RapidRAW](https://github.com/CyberTimon/RapidRAW) is an open-source, lightweight, GPU-accelerated RAW image editor designed by Timon Käch. The `raweditor` module and non-destructive RAW editing workflow in TStar have been inspired by the capabilities and design of RapidRAW.
*Important License Note:* RapidRAW is distributed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. 

We thank these teams and developers for their immense contributions to the open-source image processing and astrophotography communities!
